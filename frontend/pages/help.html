Help & Support

A quick guide to how Meeting Avoider AI actually works today.

Scheduling a meeting

From the Dashboard or sidebar, open Meetings → Add Meeting, fill in title, date, time, link, and participants, then save. It'll appear under Upcoming, Today, or Past depending on its date.

Finding your meetings

The Meetings tab has three views — Upcoming, Today, and Past — switchable via the tabs at the top of that page. Each meeting card has Join, Edit, and Delete actions.

Generating an AI summary

Open Meeting History → Generate AI Summary. Paste a transcript, upload a .txt file, or upload an audio recording, then click Summarize. You'll get an executive summary, key points, decisions, action items, deadlines, risks, and next steps — plus a chat box to ask questions about that specific meeting.

Using the Chrome extension

Load the extension from the meeting-avoider-extension folder (see the project README), log in with your account, then capture or paste a transcript and send it — it'll open the summarization page with everything pre-filled.

Profile & account

Your profile (personal info, password, meeting stats, notification preference) is on the Profile page. Your profile photo is stored in your browser only, so it won't follow you to a different device or browser.
