Features

Everything below is a real, working part of the application.

AI Meeting Summaries

Paste a transcript, upload a .txt file, or upload audio. Get an executive summary, detailed summary, decisions, deadlines, risks, and next steps — not one long paragraph.

Chrome Extension Integration

Capture or paste a transcript from your browser. It's sent straight to your account, a meeting is created automatically, and the summary page opens pre-filled.

AI Chat Per Meeting

Ask "who was assigned the frontend?" or "what deadline was set?" — answered strictly from that meeting's own transcript and summary.

Action Item Extraction

Action items, decisions, and deadlines are pulled out automatically and shown as their own dedicated cards, not buried in prose.

Meeting History

Every scheduled meeting and every generated summary is saved and browsable — upcoming, today, and past, all in one place.

AI Insights

Your dashboard surfaces real patterns from your own data — most discussed topics, most frequent meeting category, and more — only shown when there's enough data to support them.
